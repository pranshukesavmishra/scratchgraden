📋 Instructions

Dragon's Keep — a fantasy platformer.
A young wizard is trapped in an ancient castle. Three magic crystals will break the spell — but a restless ghost guards the halls, and a sleeping dragon waits at the far tower.

Let's build a game where the wizard explores the castle, collects the crystals, dodges the ghost, and reaches the dragon to win.

Hint — build it step by step
  • Move the Wizard with the arrow keys, and animate the walk with its costumes.
  • Make each Crystal disappear and add +1 to a score when the wizard touches it.
  • Make the Ghost chase the wizard (point towards, then move).
  • If the wizard touches the ghost, send them back to the start.
  • When the score reaches 3, the Dragon celebrates — You Win!
  • Stretch: add lives, a second castle level, and a start screen.
